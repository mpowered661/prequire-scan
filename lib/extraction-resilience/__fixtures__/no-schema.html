Cold Brew Subscriptions

Harbor Coffee Roasters ships cold brew concentrate from our Portland roastery on the first Tuesday of every month. Each shipment is roasted the week it ships, so the concentrate arrives inside its peak window.

Plans and pricing

Monthly price: $28. Shipping cost: $0 for orders over $25.

Concentrate yield: 12 servings per bottle.

How it works

Choose a plan, pick a grind, and we handle the rest. Pause or cancel any time from your account page.

Where we ship

We currently ship to Oregon, Washington, and Idaho.
