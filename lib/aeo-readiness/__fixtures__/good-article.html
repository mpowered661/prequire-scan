What Is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

By Jane Smith · May 27, 2026

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring content so that AI engines like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity can find, parse, and cite it accurately. Unlike traditional SEO, which targets ten blue links, AEO targets the direct answer box.

Why AEO Matters

As more users turn to AI assistants for information, the first page of results is increasingly a single cited answer. Brands that appear in those citations capture attention at the moment of intent—without requiring a click.

A well-optimized AEO page answers a question directly, structures supporting content in scannable sections, includes FAQ markup, and establishes author authority through schema and backlinks.

Core AEO Techniques

The most effective AEO techniques include writing direct answers in the first paragraph, using structured headers that mirror search queries, adding FAQ schema markup, and ensuring AI crawlers can access the page without JS challenges or bot blocks.

Beyond technical accessibility, content authority matters: AI engines weigh citation counts, author credentials, and topical consistency when deciding which sources to surface in generated answers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO optimizes for search engine rankings in a list of links. AEO optimizes for being cited directly in AI-generated answers, where a single source typically wins the entire user interaction.
